FBCNN - Flexible Blind JPEG Artifacts Removal
=============================================

Models:   fbcnn_color_blind, fbcnn_gray_blind, fbcnn_color_flex, fbcnn_gray_flex
Source:   https://github.com/jiaxi-jiang/FBCNN
Weights:  https://github.com/jiaxi-jiang/FBCNN/releases/tag/v1.0
          (fbcnn_color.pth, fbcnn_gray.pth)
Paper:    J. Jiang, K. Zhang, R. Timofte, "Towards Flexible Blind JPEG Artifacts
          Removal", ICCV 2021.

License:  Apache-2.0
Copyright (c) Jiaxi Jiang, Kai Zhang, Radu Timofte.

The ONNX models in onnx/fbcnn/ are produced from the official Apache-2.0
pretrained weights by export_fbcnn.py. The conversion replicates FBCNN's inner
forward without its internal multiple-of-8 padding so the graph is dynamic in
height/width (input must therefore be a multiple of 8, as for DRUNet). FBCNN
operates on [0,1] RGB / luma, matching the --vpp-onnx host normalisation; no
value rescale is baked in. The derived models carry the same Apache-2.0 terms.
