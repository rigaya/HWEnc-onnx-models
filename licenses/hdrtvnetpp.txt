HDRTVNet++ ONNX Models

Source: https://github.com/xiaom233/HDRTVNet-plus
Paper: Towards Efficient SDRTV-to-HDRTV by Learning from Image Formation
License: MIT

Converted from the official pretrained weights:
- AGCM.pth -> hdrtvnetpp_agcm_dynamic.onnx
- Ensemble_AGCM_LE.pth -> hdrtvnetpp_ensemble_dynamic.onnx

The exported models take gamma-encoded BT.709 RGB in 0..1 and output
HDRTV/PQ-domain BT.2020 RGB in 0..1. The generated models.json entries set
colormatrix_out=bt2020 for QSVEnc / NVEnc / VCEEnc --vpp-onnx.
